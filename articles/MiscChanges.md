# Misc Changes

*TEXT Changes*

Most story dialogue has been altered. For the most part, it is just flavor and can be safely ignored. It is sort of an "abridged" version of the story, add silly dialogue without "ruining" or "deviating" from the main story too much. I found it funny when writing it, it is very likely you will not feel the same. That's ok.

Text will be colored if it is actually important in some way. 

Route and location signs have the area's wild and SOS encounters written on them.

Many trainers will explain obscure mechanics or interesting strategies. Try speaking with them after battles!

Almost every Move/Ability/Item description has been rewritten or given more detail, including specific percentages for secondary effects, niche interactions, or hidden effects that certain combinations have. (Stomp increasing in power if the enemy uses Minimize, Surf being able to hit opponents using Dive, etc.)
There are LOTS of changes here, but here is one example. 
Drizzle (Original): The Pokémon makes it rain when it enters a battle.
Drizzle (New): The Pokémon makes it rain when it enters a battle. Lasts 5 turns. 8 turns if holding a Damp Rock. Rain boosts Water moves by 50%, and weakens Fire moves by 50%

In battle, certain important texts are colored for clarity. Ex: "Its a Critical Hit!" is colored bright Orange. "Its not very effective..." is colored a light blue.

The trainer passport contains spoiler free levels for upcoming major bosses, so that you can make your own levelcaps if you desire, or check the existing ones if you are playing with the optional Hard Level Caps on.

*EXP/EV Changes* 

Due to the increase of the amount of pokemon, as well as their increase in level, EXP rewards have been reduced to ~60% of normal. (Even with this massive decrease, you will still level up much faster than in the base game, so don't worry about needing to grind, it shouldn't be nessecary, in addition, Rare Candies are available.) 

An optional Hard Level Cap option is included as well for people who like to play with that. 

Rare Candies are available in every Pokemon Center for purchase.

EVs have been removed. The goal of this is to make teambuilding for the player simpler, as well as lowering the overall power level of enemy trainers by reducing min/maxing and promoting a higher emphasis on base stats. Mixed attackers are more viable. Grinding for EVS or even having to manage them with berries/vitamins is still a tedious process, and while I find EVs healthy in the base competitive scene, I think the balancing consequences of their removal are more interesting in a single player experience than the alternative.
